Our Recycling Partners

Elite Suppliers at the Top of the Recycling Chain.

None of what we do would be possible without partners who have mastered the recovery of hard-to-recycle plastics.

♻️ Mixed Multilayer Film

Heat-fused multilayer plastic film is notoriously difficult to recycle. HydroBlox converts this otherwise landfill-bound material into high-performance drainage product.

🍬 Post-Consumer Flexibles

Candy wrappers, chip bags, and other flexible packaging — materials that flow right through traditional recycling sorting — become HydroBlox planks.

🏗️ Industrial Purge & Offcuts

Manufacturing purge material, mixed bales, HDPE pipe offcuts, and PP bags that have no conventional recycling outlet become the feedstock for the recycled-plastic drainage plank.

Pressure-fed drainage that runs on level ground. No aggregate, no fabric, no slope. 18 years, installed nationwide.
