Specs

No pump. No pitch. Works flat.

The mechanism behind drainage that runs on level ground.

Conveyance

Water pushes water.

A French drain needs a slope because gravel doesn't move water — gravity does. Gravel just gets out of the way.

HydroBlox moves water itself. A plank fills, and pressure carries the water into the next one. That's why it runs at 0.0% grade.

Fig 1 · Pressure-fed conveyance Installed grade 0.0%
A plank fills, and hydrostatic pressure carries the water into the next — no slope, no pump. No aggregate · no filter fabric · no perforated pipe
Clogging

Nothing to clog.

The two components that fail in a French drain — aggregate and geotextile — aren't in the system. No filter to blind, no voids to silt.

Fig 2 · Why French drains fail Cross-section
Gravel silts and fabric blinds over, so the water stops. HydroBlox has neither — nothing to clog. Not to scale
