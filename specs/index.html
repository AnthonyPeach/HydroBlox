The short version

Three line items off the section detail.

HydroBlox replaces the aggregate-and-fabric underdrain: no stone envelope, no geotextile, no minimum slope. That removes three materials from the detail and the failure modes that come with them — the fabric that blinds over, the aggregate that silts, the pitch that has to be graded in.
