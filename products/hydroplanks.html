Products · Core Product

HydroPlanks.

The plank that moves water on level ground — no aggregate, no fabric, no slope.

What it is

A permanent alternative to stone and pipe.

A French drain moves water into gravel that eventually clogs. HydroPlanks move water through a high-void thermoplastic structure using natural pressure — plank to plank, in any direction, even on perfectly level ground. There is no aggregate to pack and no geotextile to blind. Nothing to clog.

Once it's in the ground it stays there: zero maintenance, no jetting, no replacement cycle. Every plank is made from 100% recycled film that would otherwise reach the landfill.

Specifications

The numbers that go in the drawing set.

  • Dimensions: 9" wide × 2" thick × 90" or 40" long
  • Weight: 90" = 30 lbs · 40" = 14 lbs (approx.)
  • Compression: 40,000 lbs/sq.ft.
  • Flow rate: 14.25 gal per 40" plank per minute
  • Material: 100% recycled thermoplastic film
  • Orientation: install vertically or horizontally — no slope required
