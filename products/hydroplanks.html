HydroBlox products deliver highly effective, long-lasting drainage. Designed for simplicity and durability — permanent performance from day one.

Core Product

HydroPlanks

A permanent, superior alternative to traditional drainage. The high-void structure offers greater drainage capacity and structural stability than stone or pipe — and installs easily on completely level ground.

  • Dimensions: 9" wide × 2" thick × 90" or 40" long
  • Weight: 90" = 30 lbs · 40" = 14 lbs (approx.)
  • Compression: 40,000 lbs/sq.ft.
  • Flow rate: 14.25 gal per 40" per minute
  • 100% recycled plastic construction
  • Install vertically or horizontally — no slope needed
