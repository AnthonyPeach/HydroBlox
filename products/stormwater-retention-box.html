Stormwater Retention Box.

Detention, conveyance, and infiltration in one recycled-plastic system — no stone envelope, no fabric.

What it is

Stormwater management for commercial and municipal sites.

Built from the same pressure-fed plank as the rest of the system, scaled for warehouses, parking lots, and municipal infrastructure. It handles large volumes and needs no maintenance over its full lifetime — there is no stone envelope to settle and no geotextile to blind.

The recycled-plastic structure supports NPDES / MS4 permit documentation, and ties into existing pipe and structures through the Transition Box.

What it does

One structure, three jobs.

  • Scales to any site size or volume requirement
  • Integrates with existing systems via the Transition Box
  • Zero maintenance after installation
  • Exceeds the capacity of traditional stone or pipe systems
  • Supports NPDES / MS4 permit documentation
  • Built from 100% recycled thermoplastic film
