Transition Boxes.

Where the plank system meets conventional pipe.

What it does

Spec it into a retrofit.

Most projects aren't greenfield. Transition boxes connect HydroBlox to existing pipe, structures, and outfalls — so you can add it to a retrofit without redesigning everything downstream.

The standard transition box is a 12" × 12" hollow unit, flanged on one end, that ties the plank system into solid pipe for a daylight outlet or diversion — use one at any connection to conventional pipe. A larger plank-built stormwater box (~52 gal, ~130 lb) replaces a drywell for downspout retention and sump discharge.
