Corners & Junctions.

Turn a corner or tie two runs together — the planks simply make contact.

What it is

Rigid planks. Simple junctions.

HydroBlox planks don't bend, and they don't need to. To change direction or join two runs, you butt or overlap the plank ends so they make contact — pressure carries the water straight across the junction, the same way it moves down the run.

No flexible parts, no molded corner fittings. Fasten the contact with stainless-steel screws or landscape spikes, keep the panels touching for continuity, and backfill with native soil.

How it works

Make contact. Keep the flow.

  • Rigid planks — no flexible parts and no special corner fittings
  • Turn a corner by butting or overlapping the plank ends
  • Cross two runs at a junction where the planks make contact
  • Fasten with stainless-steel screws or landscape spikes
  • Keep panels in contact for continuous, pressure-fed flow
  • Same 100% recycled plank as every HydroBlox run
