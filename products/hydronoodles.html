Products · Specialty

HydroNoodles.

The flexible drain for curves, corners, and the spaces a rigid plank can't reach.

What it is

Drainage that bends around the geometry.

Some drains have to follow a curve — behind a retaining wall, along a paver run, around a corner a rigid plank can't turn. HydroNoodles flex to the shape while moving water the same way HydroPlanks do: natural pressure, no aggregate, no fabric, nothing to clog.

Same recycled thermoplastic, same permanent install. They connect directly to standard HydroPlanks, so a single run can go from straight to curved without a transition.
