Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is HydroBlox right for my yard? +
HydroBlox works on nearly any residential drainage problem — standing water, wet basements, soggy yards, shifting retaining walls, and failed French drains. The system is especially effective where gravity-dependent solutions have failed, because it uses water pressure rather than slope.
How is HydroBlox different from a French drain? +
French drains rely on slope, gravel, and perforated pipe — all of which clog, crush, and fail over time. HydroBlox uses natural water pressure and a no-clog high-void structure. It installs on level ground, supports 40,000 lbs/sq.ft., requires zero maintenance, and is made entirely from recycled plastics.
Does HydroBlox support NPDES and MS4 permits? +
HydroBlox supports NPDES / MS4 permit documentation for residential, commercial, and municipal projects across the United States. These permits regulate the discharger, not the product — HydroBlox gives your engineer the performance data to support the filing.
Where can I purchase HydroBlox? +
HydroBlox is available through a network of regional distributors across the US, Canada, and Puerto Rico — plus nationwide through Home Depot Pro for contractors. See our full distributor list on the Where to Buy page, or contact us directly.
Does HydroBlox require maintenance after installation? +
No. HydroBlox is a true install-and-forget system. Unlike perforated pipe (which requires periodic jetting) or gravel trenches (which silt up), HydroBlox's no-clog design requires zero maintenance over its lifetime.
Are technical specifications or CAD files available? +
Yes — engineers, designers, and contractors can access full technical specifications, CAD files, installation guides, and professional resources at HydroBloxTech.com.
