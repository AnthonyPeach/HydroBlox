About HydroBlox

18 Years in the Ground

For 18 years, HydroBlox has been the trusted provider of drainage solutions for commercial and industrial projects. Our unique planks move water from wet areas to dry ones using natural water pressure — even on perfectly level ground.

Once installed, HydroBlox is permanent, zero-maintenance, and made entirely from 100% recycled plastics.

How It Works

Why HydroBlox Outperforms Every Alternative

Four reasons contractors, engineers, and homeowners make the permanent switch.

01
No Clogging

Nothing to Clog by Design

HydroBlox's high-void structure keeps silt out and water flowing permanently. No jetting, no service calls, no replacement. Ever.

02
Works Flat

Natural Pressure Replaces Gravity

HydroBlox uses water pressure to push water from plank to plank — in any direction, even on perfectly level ground.

03
Crush-Resistant

40,000 lbs/sq.ft. — Permanently

Supports vehicle traffic, heavy equipment, and deep installations without deforming — unlike perforated pipe which collapses under load.

04
Eco-First

100% Recycled — Better Performance

Every pound comes from plastic otherwise headed to the landfill — multilayer film, mixed bales, candy wrappers — transformed into the recycled-plastic drainage plank.
