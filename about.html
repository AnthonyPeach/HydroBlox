About HydroBlox

18 Years Proving the Gravel Was the Problem.

For 18 years, HydroBlox has delivered pressure-fed drainage that professionals trust and homeowners depend on — no gravel, no fabric, no slope.

Our Story

Built on a Simple Insight.

Traditional drainage fails because it relies on gravity, gravel, and pipe — materials that clog, crush, and deteriorate over time. HydroBlox was founded on a different premise: use natural water pressure instead of slope, and build with materials that truly last.

For 18 years, HydroBlox has been the trusted provider of drainage solutions for commercial and industrial projects. Our unique planks move water from unwanted wet areas to dry ones using natural water pressure. As water fills one plank, it's effortlessly pushed to the next — making installation easy even on level ground.

Once installed, HydroBlox is a permanent, low-maintenance solution to stormwater issues at any scale.
